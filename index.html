Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 04, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile application Just Scan It (the "Application"). The Application is provided as a free service. Aside from third-party advertising partners, the developer does not collect, store, or share any personal data or usage metrics.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not personally collect, store, transmit, or monitor any personal data or personal identifiers through the Application. There are no user account systems, registration requirements, or database interactions that record your activity.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of this Application, we display banner advertisements using Google AdMob. To deliver relevant and targeted advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain technical information from your device. This data processing is subject to European data protection regulations (GDPR).

The identifiers and processing metrics tracked by Google AdMob may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Android's Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA).
  • Technical Information: IP address, device manufacturer, device model, operating system version, and system language settings.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Metrics indicating which banner ads were displayed, clicked, or dismissed.

3. GDPR Compliance and Legal Bases

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal bases relied upon for processing this data include:

  • Consent: Where required by law, Google AdMob will request your explicit permission to serve personalized advertisements based on tracking identifiers. You can withdraw or adjust this consent at any time via your device's operating system settings.
  • Legitimate Interests: Basic ad serving, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and internal performance diagnostics are processed based on the legitimate business interests of our monetization partners.

4. Your Data Rights

Under the GDPR, users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) possess specific privacy rights regarding their data. Because the Application does not hold or store your personal identifiers on private servers, any automated tracking data rests with our third-party infrastructure partner. You can manage these parameters directly:

  • Opt-Out of Personalized Tracking: Restrict ad tracking profiles within your device's native operating system settings (e.g., via "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).
  • Google Terms: Review how your information is managed by visiting the Google Advertising & Privacy Terms.

5. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or amend this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published directly on this web page. Your continued use of the Application following updates signifies your acknowledgment of the amended policy terms.
