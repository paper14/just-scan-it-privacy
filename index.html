Just Scan It: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the Just Scan It app does with your information. Just Scan It is made by NiPaKi IT Solutions ("we").

The short version

  • Your scans stay on your phone. We never receive them, and they are never used for ads.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics of our own.
  • Saved PDFs are searchable: the app reads the text on each page on your phone. Your pages and their text are never sent to us or to Google.
  • The app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the scan list and the preview screen, provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google collects some information from your device, such as its advertising ID and IP address. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first.

What the app handles, and where it stays

Camera images. When you scan, the camera captures pictures of your documents. The app processes them on your phone (cropping, rotating and the Auto and Black & White filters).

Text in your scans. When you save or share a PDF, the app reads the text on each page (text recognition, also called OCR) and adds it to the PDF as a hidden layer, so you can search, select and copy it. This happens on your phone. The recognized text is kept only inside the PDF; the app does not store it anywhere else.

Your scans. Saved scans (the page images, the PDF or JPEG files, and the name you give each scan) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them. They are deleted when you delete the scan in the app, or when you uninstall the app.

Copies you choose to save. If the "Also save to Downloads" or "Also save to Photos" option is on when you save, the app puts a copy of the PDF or JPEG files in your phone's Downloads folder or photo gallery (on iPhone: the Files app or the Photos app). Those copies are then managed by you and your phone like any other file, and other apps on your phone can open them. Deleting a scan in Just Scan It does not delete these copies.

Files you share. When you tap Share, the app hands the files to the app you pick (for example email or a chat app). What happens to them next is up to you and that app.

Settings. The app remembers whether the "Also save to" option is on, and your choice of light or dark theme. These stay on your phone.

Advertising

The scan list ("My scans") and the preview screen each show a banner ad at the bottom, served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. Ads are never shown while you scan.

To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information and sends it to Google. This can include your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), information about your device and the app, and how you interact with the ads. It does not include your scans, their names or any other content you create in the app.

Google uses this information as described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy also applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices.

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it, before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time with ⋮ → Privacy & ads on the main screen.
  • Personalised ads. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone, see Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • No ads at all. Ads need an internet connection; with your phone offline, no ad is requested and no ad information is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or receive your scans or any other personal information. The app has no server of its own.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • We do not sell your information. The only information that leaves your phone through the app (other than files you choose to share) is what the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google, described above, and the technical information Google's scanner and text recognition send to Google, described under "Scanning and text recognition from Google" below. None of it includes your scans or their text.

Security

  • Your scans are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. They are also protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and storage encryption.
  • Your scans are never sent over the internet by the app, so they cannot be intercepted on the way.
  • The information the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Scans stay on your phone until you delete them in the app (⋮ → Delete on My scans) or uninstall the app, which deletes them all.
  • Files prepared for sharing are kept briefly in the app's private storage and are removed the next time you share, or when you uninstall the app.
  • Copies saved to Downloads or Photos are yours to keep or delete, like any other file on your phone. Deleting a scan in the app or uninstalling it does not remove them.
  • Ad information is kept and deleted by Google, as described in policies.google.com/technologies/retention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time (see "Your choices" above).
  • There is no account and we have no server, so we hold no data about you that you would need to ask us to delete.

Scanning and text recognition from Google

On Android, the scanning screen (finding the page edges and capturing the pages) is provided by Google's ML Kit document scanner, part of Google Play services on your phone. On iPhone, it is provided by Apple's VisionKit, which is part of iOS.

Reading the text for searchable PDFs uses Google's ML Kit text recognition, which is built into the app on both Android and iPhone.

Both ML Kit features work on your phone. According to Google, your page images and the text found in them are never sent to Google. ML Kit does send Google technical information about how these features are working, which Google uses for diagnostics and to maintain and improve ML Kit. This can include your phone's maker, model and system version, the app's name and version, how long recognition took, the size and format of the images, error codes, and an identifier for this installation of the app. Google says the identifier is not intended to identify you or your phone. The information is sent over an encrypted connection, Google does not pass it on to third parties, and ML Kit may also download updates from Google from time to time. Details: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan your documents. The app asks for it the first time it is needed.
  • Internet: used to show ads, and by Google's ML Kit to send the technical information described under "Scanning and text recognition from Google". The app's own code never uses it.
  • Advertising ID (Android): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described under Advertising.
  • Add to Photos (iPhone): only to save copies of your scans to your photos when you ask it to.
  • Storage (Android): the app does not ask for access to your files. On Android 9 and older, where saving a copy directly would need that access, the app opens the system's save screen instead and you choose where the copy goes.

You can turn permissions off at any time in your phone's settings. The app will tell you if it needs one to do what you asked.

Children

Just Scan It is a general tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
